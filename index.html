(01)Our story

We’re a tiny kitchen that believes a cookie should be crisp at the edge, molten in the middle and finished with a pinch of flaky salt. We brown the butter until it smells like toffee, rest every dough overnight and bake in batches small enough to watch. Nothing rushed, nothing frozen, nothing you can’t pronounce.

(02)The process

From dough to door, in four slow steps.

Keep scrolling →

01

Brown the butter

Slowly, until the milk solids turn hazelnut-gold and the kitchen smells like toffee. It’s the backbone of every cookie.

02

Let it rest

At least 48 hours in the cold. The flour hydrates, the sugars deepen and the flavour turns rich and caramelly.

03

Bake small

A handful of trays at a time, pulled the moment the edges set and the centres still wobble.

04

Packed warm

Boxed by hand, tied with twine and handed over or sent out the same day. Warm, if you’re quick.
